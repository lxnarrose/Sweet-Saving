Sweet Saving is a practice project done just for fun, and as a way to solve or make a common problem, finances, a **_little_** more enjoyable.
# Functionalities
* Creation of savings-oriented goals for short-, long-term or medium-term objectives
  - Add or withdraw money from the savings amount at your convenience, or if for some reason you had to take some money out of the amount saved.
* It is planned at some point in the development to integrate an expense tracker.
# How does it work?
The operation is very simple: 
1. In the first bar you enter the amount to save, in the second bar the reason for saving.
2. Once the goal is created, you can start adding or withdrawing the money you have saved, and in case you meet the goal or for some reason have to cancel it, you can delete it.

All of this is saved within the browser, so it is recommended (in case you clear your history), not to delete your cookies to avoid losing the information.
